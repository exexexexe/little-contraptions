Five Ways To Say It

one ordinary day · five registers that will not shut up
Five registers, and each is a different shape on the page rather than the same verse in a different hat: the shanty has a response line and something to haul on, the chronicle does not rhyme at all, the pirate is two couplets and a shout, the sonnet-ish one closes on a couplet, and the detective refuses to rhyme on principle. Every line was written for this page. Nothing here reproduces a real shanty, chantey, ballad or song — the traditional repertoire is full of perfectly good verses and none of them are used here. Your sentence is read for a noun and a verb and woven in; the same sentence gives the same verse until you ask again. ← back to the cabinet
