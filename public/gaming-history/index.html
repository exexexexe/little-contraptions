Gaming, On This Day

real dates · nothing invented
Everything on this page is a real, dated event. Nothing here is invented, and no date has been rounded or guessed to make a better anniversary. An entry needs a firm day to be in the list at all, which is why a good deal of important history — the 1983 crash, the Commodore 64’s launch, Spacewar! being finished at MIT — is not here: those have months and years, not days. Dates are the original-territory release unless an entry says otherwise, because that is the day the thing actually happened; where the better-known date belongs to another region, the entry gives both. Where there is a caveat — a staggered launch, a disputed day — it is printed rather than hidden. Every claim is one you can check. ← back to the cabinet
