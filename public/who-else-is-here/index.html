Where from

    What this keeps. Your address is used once, on the server, to ask a geocoder which city it is — and is then thrown away. It is never written down. What is stored is a city name and a latitude and longitude rounded to one decimal place, which is about eleven kilometres, so the dot is the city and not you. You are counted by a random id your own browser invented and keeps in its own storage; it says nothing about you and exists only so that reloading does not count twice. Rows are deleted after twenty-four hours.
