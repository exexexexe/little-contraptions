        How the clip works. The audio really does start at one second and grow: the same thirty-second preview is played from the top and stopped at the window you have earned, so asking for more time genuinely uncovers more music rather than revealing a bar that was already playing. Guess sooner and it scores higher. Where it comes from. Clips are the official previews from Deezer’s own API, fetched at play time and played from their servers — nothing is hosted or re-served here. Every track in the pool was checked against that API before being included.
