The stations, the DJs and everything they say are invented for this cabinet — no real broadcaster, station identity or presenter is being reproduced. With sound off, the running order is invented too and is marked as such. With sound on, the tracks are real recordings streamed from the Internet Archive's netlabel collection, released under Creative Commons licences, and each one is credited with its own title, artist and licence rather than the made-up ones. ← back to the cabinet
