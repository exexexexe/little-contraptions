Eighteen dilemmas, several of them the classical thought experiments in their standard form — the lever and the footbridge, the pond, the machine, the veil. The tally underneath is a record of which kind of reason you reached for, not a verdict: these traditions disagree with each other in print and have done for two centuries, and a page cannot settle that.
