Writing today’s card…

This recipe did not exist before you asked for it. A language model writes it fresh against the four choices above. It is not taken from a cookbook, it has not been tested by anyone, and nobody has eaten it. Read it the way you would read a recipe from a stranger: the method will usually be sound and the seasoning is yours to argue with.

What the model is told it may not do. No health or nutrition claims, no calorie figures, no canning, curing, fermenting or foraging instructions, and meat, poultry and eggs cooked through with a temperature given. If a card ever breaks one of those, it is a bug and the recipe is wrong — trust your own kitchen sense over the card.

One card per day per set of choices: it is kept in this browser, so today’s dinner is today’s dinner however many times you come back. “Another one” overwrites it deliberately.
