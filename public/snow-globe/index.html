The Nostalgia Snow Globe

pick it up · shake it · put it down again
—
Three things in here were built somewhere else in this cabinet and are borrowed rather than rewritten: the falling snow is the Elsewhere Almanac's particle field, the ambience is Room Tone's Web Audio engine, and the snow that settles at the bottom and gets thrown up when you shake is real rigid-body physics under Matter.js. The dioramas are drawn a pixel at a time into a 128×128 buffer and scaled by a whole number, so nothing inside the glass can be smooth by accident. The carol is Jingle Bells (1857) or Silent Night (1818), both long out of copyright, arranged here for four oscillators and played live — there is no recording of anything on this page, and no performance by anybody has been sampled. ← back to the cabinet
