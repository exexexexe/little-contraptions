Photographs come from Pexels through their API, under the Pexels licence, and every one carries its photographer's name and a link to their page whether or not the licence insists. The key stays on the server; the search terms are a fixed list here rather than anything you type, so this cannot become a free image search running on someone else's quota. Nothing is pulled from Reddit, Instagram or anyone's social upload. The sentence under each photograph was written by this page and is not a caption the photographer wrote, a title, or a claim about where the picture was taken — it is a mood, and it belongs to the page rather than to the place.
