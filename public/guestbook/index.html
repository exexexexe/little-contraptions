Everything on this page was typed by a stranger and is stored on the server, so anyone can read it. There is no account and no name behind any of it — the book knows your entries apart from everyone else's by a random string your own browser invented, which is all it knows. Messages are capped at 280 characters, one every couple of minutes, and links are refused, because link spam is the only thing an open text box on the internet reliably attracts.
