The Quietest Day

fewest entries most · click any day to read what is on it
What this actually measures. The colours count how many entries Wikipedia's editors have written for each date — not how much happened on it. A quiet day here is a day that was recorded less, which is a fact about the encyclopedia rather than a fact about history. Coverage leans heavily towards the last few centuries and the English-speaking world, and the lean is visible in the grid: the first of January is the darkest square of the year, which tells you something about record-keeping and nothing at all about the first of January.
Counts were taken from Wikimedia's on-this-day feed on 9 September 2026 and are stored beside this page, which is why the whole year appears at once. The events listed for a day you click are fetched live, so those are current. ← back to the cabinet
