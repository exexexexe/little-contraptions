The Index of Devices
That Do Not Exist

Five hundred machines, one line each: what it is called, where it comes from, and what it actually does. No plot summaries and no borrowed prose — every line was written for this page. Roughly two in five were invented for this index and exist nowhere else; those are marked in rust and can be filtered out if you only want the ones you can look up.

    Nothing in the index matches that.

    Names of works are given so you can find the original. Everything described here belongs to whoever made it; this page holds no artwork, no stills and no quoted text, only a factual sentence per device written from scratch. Entries with a rust-coloured source line are apocryphal — invented for this index, cited by nobody, and mine.
