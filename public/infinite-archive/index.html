The Infinite Archive

everything that can be written is already shelved here
Nothing here is fetched, stored, or searched: the page is constructed in your browser at the moment you ask for it. The archive is a function, not a library — the shelf, volume and page numbers are derived from what you typed, so the same request always reaches the same page, and a page nobody has requested has never been anywhere at all. The idea is an old one in literature and belongs to nobody; every word of this implementation, and every line of the text around your passage, was written here. ← back to the cabinet
