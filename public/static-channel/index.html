what you have tuned through

    Everything on this set comes from the Prelinger Archives at the Internet Archive — a collection of ephemeral film (advertising, educational, industrial and amateur) that the archive makes freely available for reuse. Nothing is downloaded or re-hosted here: the video streams straight from archive.org, and every channel links back to its item page. Films are picked at random and started somewhere in the middle, which is why you never get a beginning. They are historical documents, and some of them carry the assumptions of the year they were made.
