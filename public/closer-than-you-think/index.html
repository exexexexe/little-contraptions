Closer Than You’d Think

Every date on this page is real. Nothing is invented, nothing is rounded to make a better line, and where a date is genuinely an estimate — a pyramid, an extinction, the first tree — it says so and the arithmetic is done on the estimate rather than on a false precision. Each comparison shows the gaps as numbers and the fact you would want to check, so you do not have to take any of it on trust. Nothing is fetched; the whole list is one file. ← back to the cabinet
