Kardashev, and what comes after

Civilizations,
ranked

In 1964 the astronomer Nikolai Kardashev proposed sorting civilizations not by age, beauty or wisdom, but by one blunt number: how much energy they can lay hands on. It is a crude measure. It is also the only one that scales. Here is the whole ladder, from where we actually stand to the rungs nobody has any business drawing yet.

scroll↓

We are on the first rung, and it is not a metaphor.

Kardashev's classification: N. S. Kardashev, Transmission of Information by Extraterrestrial Civilizations, Soviet Astronomy, 1964. The continuous version used for the figure above is Carl Sagan's interpolation. Solar luminosity is the IAU nominal value. World energy use is annual primary energy from the Energy Institute's Statistical Review. Every figure on this page is either one of those published values or arithmetic on them, and the arithmetic is printed next to the result.
Rungs beyond the second are labelled speculative because they are: no engineering exists for them, and the numbers describe what the physics would demand, not what anyone can do.
