The Bouncing Sequencer

Put a peg where you want a note

Tap anywhere to drop a peg
Balls fall on their own

Every peg is tuned by height — near the top it rings high, near the floor it is low — and all of them are on the same pentatonic scale, which is why a mess of pegs still comes out sounding like something rather than nothing. The rhythm is not sequenced anywhere. It is whatever falls out of where you put them.
