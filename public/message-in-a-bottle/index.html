How this works. A note you throw in sinks out of sight for as long as you chose, and only then can it wash up for somebody else. It is kept on the server, so these really are other visitors’ notes and yours really will outlive your visit. Nothing is attached to it — no name, no address, no id — which also means it cannot be edited or taken back. Nobody reads these before you do: links are refused and the length is capped, but there is no moderator.
