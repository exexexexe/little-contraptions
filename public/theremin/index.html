The Theremin

Played by not touching it

lowhigh loudquiet
Click, then move across — left to right for pitch, up and down for volume
—silent

A real theremin works by detuning: your hand is one plate of a capacitor, and moving it shifts an oscillator against a fixed one. What you hear is the difference between the two. This one has no antennae and no capacitance — the pointer does the job of both hands — but the awkward part is faithful enough, which is that there are no frets, keys or stops anywhere, and every note in between is available whether you wanted it or not.
