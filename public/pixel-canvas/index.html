Ninety-six by sixty-four cells, shared by everyone who opens this page. You get eight pixels every half hour — enough to add something, not enough to take the whole thing over — and any cell can be painted over by whoever comes next, which is the point rather than a flaw. Nothing is stored about you except a random string your own browser invented, used only so the canvas knows how many of your eight are left. There is no undo and no ownership. ← back to the cabinet
