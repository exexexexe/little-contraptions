Design Your Own Country

the flag decides everything else
 
 

The country you have just made

Every part of the description is decided by a part of the flag, so this is a design tool rather than a random generator: change the charge and only the beliefs change, change the field and only the land does. Nothing here describes a real country, and the map is not a map of anywhere — the coastline is generated from your choices and the sea has no other shore. Names are assembled from invented syllables; if one happens to resemble a real place, that is the alphabet's fault and not a claim about it. ← back to the cabinet
