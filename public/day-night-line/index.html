The terminator is computed here, in your browser, from the date and the standard low-precision solar position — the sun's declination and the equation of time — which is accurate to well under a degree, or roughly a minute of sunrise. It is not fetched from anywhere and nothing about you is sent anywhere; the city times come from your own browser's time-zone database. The shading is geometric daylight and takes no account of atmospheric refraction, terrain, or the fact that the sun has a width, so a place drawn right on the line is genuinely ambiguous rather than wrongly drawn.
